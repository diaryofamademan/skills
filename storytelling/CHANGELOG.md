# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-23

First release.

- The eight-step core procedure: name the job and audience, choose the arc, build the structure, construct the hook, write scenes against the function test, design the ending, hand off scene cards, and close the feedback loop.
- The evidence base in `references/lessons.md`: forty-four lessons across the hook, structure and arcs, scenes, endings, titles and packaging, and sharing — each labeled measured, practitioner, framing, platform-declared, or observed, with its source and sample.
- The scene-card method in `references/storyboard-method.md`: six fields per card, the function test applied card by card, and the handoff rules for producers and channel operators.
- One public worked example: a second-person founder story taken through the whole procedure, with its hook, three scene cards, and its ending.
- The standing rules wired in from the first version: every lesson carries its label in any output that uses it, framing is never presented as data, the user's own measured results outrank every imported lesson, and no number enters without its source and sample.
- Built as the narrative-craft companion to the channel operator skill, which calls this skill at its script and storyboard stages and takes the scene cards as its production input.
