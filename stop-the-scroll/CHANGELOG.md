# Changelog

## 0.1.4 — 2026-11-15

The story's third test added: the logic pass. Every physical claim in a story post must hold together under a plain reading. Earned by the author's catch on the punchy hike draft, which placed Herbie at the back of the line and then claimed nobody could pass him; the worked example in the patterns reference is corrected to the book's own mechanics (Herbie walks in the middle; the troop arrives when he arrives). The pass is stated in the procedure, the principles file, and the checklist. No other changes.

## 0.1.3 — 2026-11-15

The sea-of-100 test extended to the story post, from the same launch's analogy pass: the story must stand out in a sea of 100 stories, and a boring story is raw material to make exciting, not an excuse. The four-part treatment is recorded in the procedure, the checklist, and the patterns reference (name the character, one concrete detail, the stuck beat on its own short line, the backwards turn ending on an image), worked on the hike story from The Goal. No other changes.

## 0.1.2 — 2026-11-15

The stand-alone rule extended from the hook and the call to action to every load-bearing post, the story explicitly, from the author's analogy pass on the fix-the-bottleneck launch: a story post that leans on the thread for its setup is rewritten with the setup inside the post. The thread architecture is now stated in the procedure and the patterns reference: hook, story, the author's example, stat, big win, call to action, with transitions carried by bridge lines inside the posts. No other changes.

## 0.1.1 — 2026-11-14

Principle 5 sharpened with the stats rule, from the author's statistic pick for the fix-the-bottleneck launch the same night: a statistic must multiply or reduce (increase money, increase efficiency, reduce waste, increase speed) and pass three tests — one visible number, no reader math, and a change shown rather than a state. The rejected and winning candidate statistics are recorded as the worked examples in references/the-13-principles.md. No other changes.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-14

First build. Published briefly as one-in-a-hundred and renamed to stop-the-scroll the same night at the author's direction, before any launch: the name should state what the skill does to the reader, not the odds it beats. AIDA with the ends hardened: thirteen principles in five groups (hook, body, call to action, formatting, drafting discipline), two tests applied to each end (sea-of-100 and stand-alone), an output contract for build and review runs, and the worked case study of the fourteen-version launch session the principles were distilled from. Boundaries against the voice skills (sound) and the comms skill (communications decisions) are stated in the limits section.
