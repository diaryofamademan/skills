# Changelog

## 0.1.3 — 2026-11-21

Sibling calls wired, under the playbook rule: a skill calls the sibling skills its work needs, by name, at the step that needs them. The four-skill pass shaped this skill at build time, but the procedure itself named no calls — a run could complete without ever taking them. Now they are steps:

- **Step 0 calls first-principles-thinking** — the excavation decomposes the company to the decisions it has paid for, so beliefs are read off bedrock, not self-description.

- **Step 3 calls comms** — the drafted statements are tested as communication, who must repeat them and what the hearer must be able to do differently, before the gate judges them.

- **Step 5 calls stop-the-scroll, when the manifesto module fires** — a manifesto with a named public job is public writing, and its opening and invitation are built under that skill's rules.

## 0.1.2 — 2026-11-21

Icons on the vision document. The field test's one-pager shipped clean but bare; the house standard for visual work is the Hugeicons free library, and a document people are meant to keep should carry its markers.

- **The output contract names the icon library.** Section markers on the vision document carry Hugeicons (free version) icons — stroke style, one consistent weight, sparingly, and only where the icon carries the section's meaning; an icon that carries no meaning is decoration and does not ship. The field test's page was regenerated under the rule: an eye on the vision, a target on the objective, a compass on the mission, an open book on the beliefs.

## 0.1.1 — 2026-11-21

Field-test format fix, from field test 1 (a real company's vision record, filed the same day). The first filed record shipped in analyst prose — long, dense verdict sections — even though the output contract already carried a plain-style writing pass. The pass governed the vision record but never named the field-test record, so the human-read parts of the filed test escaped it.

- **Field-test records now open plain.** A filed field-test record opens with a plain section — the verdict and the headline findings in short sentences a person would say out loud — before any working detail.

- **Verdict sections are plain in their own right.** The gate results and the verdict on the skill must be written plain, not summarized plainly over dense prose. The output contract names the human-read parts of a field-test record — the opening and every verdict — as plain-style territory; working detail below the opening may stay as dense as the evidence needs. A checklist item carries the requirement into the pre-delivery pass.

- **The standalone vision document is now a required deliverable of every run.** The same field test made the second gap plain: the draft statements sat as lines inside a test file, and the reader expected a mission statement he could actually read — a finished piece on its own page. The output contract now requires it: a one-page document carrying the vision, the mission, the objective and milestones, and the beliefs as finished prose, delivered as a document and a PDF alongside the record, with the manifesto gate's verdict in one plain line when the module is skipped.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-21

First build. One core, three lengths: the vision as a picturable future scene compressed into a statement, the mission as the same beliefs at today's work, and a manifesto that exists only when a named public job needs it.

- **Scene before statement.** The procedure opens with an excavation — beliefs read out of decisions the company has already paid for, never brainstormed — and a time-travel step that writes the future as a concrete scene in prose before any statement language is drafted. Both steps exist because of the same finding: leaders default to abstraction ("blurry vision bias," fewer than one in ten using strong imagery), and the antidote is a scene the mind can stand in.

- **The Carton pattern as the vision's structure.** One aspiration, narrowed to a concrete quantifiable objective, broken into checkable milestones, with the scene's central image kept in the statement. Values, where named, are capped at four and stated as behaviors. Every load-bearing claim carries its source and grade in `references/the-evidence.md`.

- **The decision gate as the definition of done.** A run finishes only when the record names at least three real decisions the statements change — across hiring, firing, and money, with old defaults, enforcement owners, and cost weighed — and passes a clarity mapping test on three real roles. The gate stands on Bart, Bontis and Taggar and on Gartenberg, Prat and Serafeim: purpose alone did nothing in the evidence; purpose with clarity and changed behavior did.

- **The manifesto module, gated.** It fires only for a named public job — recruiting self-selection, movement-building, or belief-as-product — with an entry test that names the reader, the intended act, and the accepted cost before drafting. No named job, no manifesto, and the record states the skip as a deliverable.

- **Honest labels, kept in the open.** Collins and Porras enter as retrospective case lore with the Halo Effect caveat attached; the vocabulary is used, the causal claim is not. Simon Sinek is excluded by name, with the reasons stated in the skill's limits: no peer-reviewed test, discredited brain biology, selected success stories. The mission-statement meta-analyses sit in the limits as the caution the whole design answers.

- **The worked example is a public case.** SpaceX's "make life multiplanetary" read through the method — the pattern, the gate, and the standing costs of specificity, including the deadline slippage a measurable vision invites and the "scale talk" critique (Yang, 2026). Specificity is priced in the record before any objective is adopted: it buys belief because missing costs face, and the skill says so.
