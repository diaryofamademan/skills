# Fact sheet format, and a worked example

## The format

A fact sheet is written to be re-read under pressure — the night before signing, in the meeting where a claim gets repeated. Fixed sections, in this order:

1. **Header.** Subject (person, company, or both), the commitment the check serves, the depth, the sweep date, and the identity confidence in one line.
2. **Summary for the decision.** Three to eight lines, plain words: what blocks, what must be explained, what checked out. Written for a reader who reads nothing else.
3. **Identity resolution.** The anchors used, the variant set, the candidate set with its outcomes, the confidence, and the namesake log (name, source, separating fact).
4. **Screening log.** The lists searched, the names, variants, entities, and addresses screened, and the date; hits resolved at the primary list or logged as namesakes.
5. **Module findings.** One subsection per module run — entity and corporate record (procurement records included); digital estate (timeline check); the paper (contract commitments); legal record; regulatory and licensing; financial-distress signals; vendor cyber and data protection (vendor commitments); on-chain record (where addresses or contracts were supplied); token offerings (token commitments); claim verification; adverse media; the international sweep (non-US subjects); verification calls and reference checks (where run). Every item: the fact, its status, its source, the date checked.
6. **Red flags, graded.** Blocks / must be explained / note and proceed, each with the finding it rests on and the three grading questions answered in a line.
7. **Inconsistencies.** Claim quoted, record quoted, side by side. No adjectives.
8. **Gaps.** Modules not run at this depth; sources sealed, paywalled, or unreachable; anything identity resolution could not settle; the standing statement that a clean section means "nothing found in the sources swept," with the sources listed.
9. **Handoff block.** The verified facts, one per line, formatted for psychological-profiling to cite as record evidence.

## Worked example — claim verification catches a fabricated biography

The most instructive public case for this skill's claim-verification module is George Santos, whose biography was checked by reporters and institutions after his 2022 election to the US Congress — entirely from public sources, against primary sources, with the subject a public figure. It is reproduced here as method, in miniature: claim, primary source checked, status.

| Claim as made | Primary source checked | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Graduate of Baruch College (with a volleyball career) | Baruch College: no record of attendance or a degree | **Contradicted** |
| Employed at Goldman Sachs | Goldman Sachs: no record of employment | **Contradicted** |
| Employed at Citigroup | Citigroup: no record of employment | **Contradicted** |
| Ran an animal-rescue charity, Friends of Pets United | IRS records: no registered charity under that name; no filings | **Contradicted** (as a registered charity) |
| Family real-estate wealth, thirteen properties | Property and court records in the named jurisdictions | **Not found** |

The legal module, run the same way, returned outcomes rather than allegations: a federal indictment (2023), expulsion from the House (December 2023), a guilty plea to wire fraud and aggravated identity theft (August 2024), and a prison sentence (2025). Graded against any commitment — partnership, hire, investment — the sheet blocks at the identity-and-claims level before the legal record is even reached.

**What the example teaches.** First, the module worked because the claims were specific: a named college, named employers, a named charity. Specific claims can be checked; the sweep's first craft is pinning a biography's claims into checkable form. Second, every contradiction came from the institution itself — the college, the banks, the tax record — not from other reporting. Third, the pattern was the finding: one contradicted claim is an error; a biography whose checkable claims fail in every direction is a fabrication, and the fact sheet says exactly that, in statuses, without needing the word. Fourth, note what the sweep did not need: no private data, no pretexting, no contact with the subject. The public record, checked at its primaries, was sufficient — as it usually is when a check is warranted at all.
