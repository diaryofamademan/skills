## Closes and equalization

- **First close:** investing begins at minimum commitments.
- **Subsequent closes:** new LPs treated as if joined at first close; final close often within ~**12 months** (ILPA).
- **Equalization (typical three parts):**
  1. Late LP pays **catch-up capital** for prior calls (rebalanced to existing LPs).
  2. **Equalization interest** at LPA rate (EisnerAmper example **6%** on catch-up for half-year lag).
  3. P&L and ownership **true-up**; catch-up **management fee** often applies.

**Example:** First close **$50M**, **20%** called ($10M). Six months later +**$25M** LP:

- Catch-up capital: **$5M** (20% of $25M), redistributed to original LPs.
- Equalization interest: $5M × 6% × 0.5 ≈ **$150k** to existing LPs.
- Catch-up fee at 2%: $25M × 2% × 0.5 ≈ **$250k**.

Rates/mechanics from **LPA** — **CPA review**.

## Placement agents

Extend pipeline; ILPA practice (excerpt): fees borne by **manager** or fully **offset** against management fee. Verify **broker-dealer** registration where required — **lawyer review**.

## Regulation D (US private funds / startups)

| Rule | Highlights |
| --- | --- |
| **506(b)** | No general solicitation; unlimited accredited; up to 35 sophisticated non-accredited per 90 days (extra disclosure) |
| **506(c)** | General solicitation allowed if **all** purchasers accredited and **reasonable verification** (tax docs, broker letter, etc.) |
| **Both** | Restricted securities; **Form D** within **15 calendar days** of first sale; state notices; bad-actor rules |

**Accredited investor (individual, common tests):** income **$200k** / **$300k** joint (2 years + reasonable expectation); or net worth **>$1M** excl. primary residence; or Series **7/65/82** license. Entities with **>$5M** assets among other categories.

### Qualified purchaser (15 U.S.C. 80a-2(a)(51), paraphrased)

**Qualified purchaser** is a statutory investor-eligibility test for **3(c)(7)** funds (and related look-through rules). Common categories include:

| Category | Threshold (investments, per SEC rules) |
| --- | --- |
| **Natural person** | Owns **$5M+** in investments (special rules for spouses and certain shared ownership with 3(c)(7) issuers). |
| **Family company** | **$5M+** in investments; owned by related family group. |
| **Trust** | Not formed solely to buy the interest; trustee/settlor/contributors qualify as QPs. |
| **Institutional-style manager** | Person investing **$25M+** on a discretionary basis for itself or other QPs. |

The SEC may adopt implementing rules; **look-through** applies to certain feeder/fund-of-funds structures so all underlying beneficial owners must qualify. **Lawyer review** before admitting any LP to a 3(c)(7) fund.

### 3(c)(1) vs 3(c)(7) (15 U.S.C. 80a-3(c), paraphrased)

| Exemption | Investor base | Offering |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **3(c)(1)** | Generally **≤100 beneficial owners** (250 for qualifying venture capital funds under later amendments); investors need not all be qualified purchasers but must satisfy private-fund offering rules. | Classic LP fund with accredited/sophisticated LPs; no public offering. |
| **3(c)(7)** | **All** owners must be **qualified purchasers**; additional limits on total beneficial owners (historically 1999 persons rule with look-through). | Larger institutions and family offices at QP thresholds; still no public offering. |

Most emerging GP funds use **506(b)** or **506(c)** **Reg D** offerings into a **3(c)(1)** or **3(c)(7)** vehicle — the securities exemption and the Investment Company Act exemption are separate analyses (**lawyer review**).

### Placement agents (ILPA Principles 3.0, paraphrased)

- Disclose GP/placement-agent economics in diligence materials.
- **Placement agent fees** should be borne by the **fund manager**; **placement expenses** by the **fund**.
- If any placement fee is charged to the fund, **management fee should fully offset** those amounts.
- **LPAC** should review placement expenses for reasonableness.
- Confirm agent **broker-dealer registration** where required (**lawyer review**).

## Marketing Rule (SEC-registered advisers, Rule 206(4)-1)

Advertisements include many fund marketing materials to prospective LPs. Prohibitions include unsubstantiated performance, misleading cherry-picking, etc. **Gross vs net** performance rules; testimonials/endorsements need disclosures and agreements. **Compliance review** before any performance, testimonial, or broad marketing — **lawyer review**.

**506(b) caution:** public posts about a live raise can taint the exemption — human + counsel decide.
