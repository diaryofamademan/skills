## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-14

First build. Named fix-the-bottleneck at the author's direction before publication: the skill is the procedure, and the name states the job; the method it runs remains Goldratt's Theory of Constraints, credited throughout as the source. The seven-step procedure (state the goal as throughput, map and measure the chain, identify the constraint, exploit, subordinate, elevate, repeat) operationalizes Goldratt's five focusing steps for knowledge-work and agent systems, with Reinertsen's flow math, throughput accounting adapted to work in progress, a four-kind constraint taxonomy (resource, policy, hero, external), and a worked case study of an AI agent fleet. The elevate step defers design decomposition to the first-principles skill and other actors' choices to the game-theory skill; both boundaries are stated in the skill's limits section.
