# Changelog

## 0.1.2 — 2026-11-24

The camera format, completed from the first run's review:

- Labels before a colon are bold (**Question:**, **Hit:**, **Land:**).
- Anything in quotes — every line the performer says out loud — is set in italics.
- Sections are separated by two line spaces, on top of the blank line between every paragraph and item.

## 0.1.1 — 2026-11-24

Field fixes from the first run (a founder filming a collection launch the same day the skill shipped):

- The beat format gains a fifth line: **Bridge**, the spoken transition into the beat. A sheet without bridges makes the performer invent the joins live, and invented joins are where a riff stalls.
- New guardrail: **no drop in the middle.** The middle beats never announce the product, collection, or offer by name; the naming happens once, in the invitation beat, after the stories have earned it.
- The deliverable rule now carries camera spacing: one sentence per paragraph, a blank line between every paragraph and every list item. The sheet is read mid-performance, often on a second screen.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-24

First published version.

- The six-step procedure: name the job, mine the record, sort the claims, build the beats, size the sheet, attach the edit brief.
- The four-line beat format: Question, Hit, Land, Flag.
- The beat order: turn, proof of life, receipts, method, quest, invitation, callback close.
- The edit brief in its fixed five-step order: transcribe, cut, cold open, weave, verify.
- Sibling calls wired in as steps: stop-the-scroll at the mining step, comms at the claim sort, storytelling at the beat order, fundraising at the invitation beat.
- Worked example and the edit-brief template in `references/`.
