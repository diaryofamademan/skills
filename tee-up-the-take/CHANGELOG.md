# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-24

First published version.

- The six-step procedure: name the job, mine the record, sort the claims, build the beats, size the sheet, attach the edit brief.
- The four-line beat format: Question, Hit, Land, Flag.
- The beat order: turn, proof of life, receipts, method, quest, invitation, callback close.
- The edit brief in its fixed five-step order: transcribe, cut, cold open, weave, verify.
- Sibling calls wired in as steps: stop-the-scroll at the mining step, comms at the claim sort, storytelling at the beat order, fundraising at the invitation beat.
- Worked example and the edit-brief template in `references/`.
