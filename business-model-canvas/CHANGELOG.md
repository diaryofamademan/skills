# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-21

First build. One skill, two sheets: a fit test decides whether the business is still searching for its model (the Lean Canvas) or executing one it has found (the nine-block Business Model Canvas), the chosen sheet is filled from evidence with every guess labeled, and the run finishes where the sheet's value actually is — a ranked list of the riskiest assumptions, each with the cheapest test that could kill it.

- **The name is the category term.** Business Model Canvas, not Lean Canvas: the skill covers both sheets, and naming it for Maurya's variant would borrow his brand to describe half the skill. The fit test, not the title, is where the Lean sheet lives.

- **The fit test is Step 0 and its verdict is a required output field.** Three questions, in order — is the problem proven, is the solution bought repeatedly, are the economics demonstrated — with a mixed-state rule (decide by the weaker half; the model, not the product, is the unit). A searching business on the nine-block sheet invents partners and programs; a found business on the Lean sheet hides from its scale questions. The test exists so neither happens by preference.

- **The criticisms are answered inside the procedure, and kept standing in the limits.** Coes (2014) and others: static snapshot, no competition or environment, no prioritization between blocks. The skill adds an environment pass (with partners and channels read as incentive problems), a ranked assumptions list as the prioritization, and dated change triggers with a review date. The limits section says plainly that these answers are partial.

- **Ladd 2018 is carried in the open.** The one direct test — 271 accelerator teams; pitch placing moved, profit did not; only customer segmentation clearly correlated; the canvas may contain unhelpful components — sits in the evidence reference and the limits, and shapes the design: the canvas is claimed as a recording and stress-testing instrument, never as a performance intervention, and the method's weight sits on segmentation precision and the assumptions, where the evidence and the logic agree it belongs.

- **The worked example is de-identified and composite.** An invented scheduling tool for independent dental clinics, still searching: the fit test refuses the flattering sheet, the unfair-advantage block is recorded empty rather than filled, and the top assumption's test is a letter with a price on it. Shapes only, per the house rule for examples drawn from practice.
