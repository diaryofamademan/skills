# Benchmark tables

Directional anchors for pricing a media property's sponsorship inventory. There is **no audited, universal benchmark** in this market: sources rarely disclose sample size or method, and several figures are list prices rather than transacted prices. Every figure below carries its basis and its source. A property's own rate history outranks every table here.

The comparability rule, stated once and applied everywhere: **never mix bases inside one comparison.** A price per 1,000 opens and a price per 1,000 subscribers describe different things; at a 40% open rate they differ by about two and a half times for the same newsletter.

The pricing method these tables support: flat fee = CPM × (audience basis ÷ 1,000). Newsletters, podcasts, and sponsored posts are usually sold as a flat fee derived from that math, not as a metered CPM.

All sources read 2026-11-19.

## Newsletters

Dominant structure: a flat fee per send, derived from a CPM calculation (Revenews). Networks backfill unsold inventory at roughly $1.50 per click or $4 CPM (Inbox Collective, quoting Revenews), which is why direct flat-fee sales beat network backfill for quality audiences.

Dedicated sends carry the highest rates and the highest unsubscribe risk; they are labeled clearly and sent to a segment, not the whole list.

## Podcasts

Structure: CPM on downloads per episode in the first 30 days. Host-read beats produced and dynamic; mid-roll beats pre-roll beats post-roll.

| Host-read, baked-in, 30 seconds | $18–22 | Libsyn Ads |
| Dynamic episodic / back-catalog | $18–22 / $14–16 | Libsyn Ads |
| Programmatic | $12–15 | Libsyn Ads |

## YouTube and video

Structure: priced on views, not subscribers — usually the median views of recent videos, or expected 30-day views — times a niche CPM.

| Format / segment | Benchmark | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Integration (60–90 seconds), all niches | about $20–50 CPM | SponsorRadar (published deals and rate cards, not measured fees) |
| Integration, entertainment | $8–15 CPM | SponsorRadar |
| Integration, tech | $30–60 CPM | SponsorRadar |
| Integration, finance | $40–80 CPM | SponsorRadar |
| Integration, under 50K views per video | $51 CPM average | Agentio 2026 playbook, 10,000+ integrations (platform data) |
| Integration, 50K–300K views per video | $28 CPM | Agentio |
| Integration, 300K+ views per video | $22 CPM | Agentio |
| Dedicated video | 1.7–2× an integration | Aspire survey, about 1,200 creators |
| Dedicated video, higher estimate | 2–4× an integration | 1of10 via SponsorRadar |
| Shorts | 0.25–0.5× an integration; about $8–25 CPM | 1of10 / Revenue Lab via SponsorRadar |

Note the disagreement worth teaching: Agentio's platform data shows smaller channels earning *higher* CPMs, while list-price guides often imply the opposite. Platform data outranks list-price guides where they conflict. Also from Agentio: about 40% of an integration's views arrive after day 30, so the measurement window belongs in the deal terms.

## Sponsored posts and link placements (web)

| Item | Price | Basis / source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sponsored article, median quote | $570 (middle half $250–$1,290) | ESBO study, 16,625 publishers; the sample is the study's own network and over-represents sellers |
| Sponsored article by authority | $355 at DR 30–39, rising to $1,840 at DR 80+ (medians) | ESBO via BacklinkPrice |
| Guest post, bought direct from the site | $295 average | BuzzStream study, 257,267 sites |
| Guest post, bought through a vendor | $461 average | BuzzStream study, 257,267 sites |
| Homepage link | sold for a 12-month term (one market example: €320–1,300) | Lemonet publisher network |

The term convention: in this segment a 12-month term with monitoring and an equal-tier replacement remedy is standard practice. RhinoRank's per-post price includes replacement cover for 12 months if a placement comes down; LinkVouch sells placements with 12 months of monitoring and equal-tier replacement at no cost if a link dies; several sellers state the remedy as "restored, moved, or credited." A rate card in this segment states term, monitoring, and remedy beside the price (see SKILL.md step 5).

## Hybrid and affiliate norms

Commission norms cited in practitioner sources: physical products 5–15%, digital and SaaS products 20–40% (Dealvio). Network backfill economics for newsletters: about $1.50 per click or $4 CPM (Inbox Collective). The hybrid floor convention — a flat fee around 50–70% of the equivalent flat rate, plus the performance component — is practitioner practice (Dealvio; AVID Media via Everflow), not a studied number. Clause language lives in [`hybrid-clauses.md`](hybrid-clauses.md).

Sources: https://www.dealvio.co/blog/influencer-marketing-pricing-models-guide.html · https://www.everflow.io/masterclass/why-the-best-brand-deals-never-reach-your-inbox-and-what-a-publisher-agency-does-differently
