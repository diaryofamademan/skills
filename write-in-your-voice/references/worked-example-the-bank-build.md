# Worked example — the bank build this skill was learned from

The author's own production build, November 2026, for a daily content operation that drafts posts in his voice. Numbers are the build record.

## The build

- **Tier 1, calls:** 500 passages cut from 1,149 meeting transcripts (Fireflies), about 224,000 words of his speech, 2024–2026. Customer-interview blocks excluded entirely; customer-voice passages filtered by pattern after that.
- **Tier 1, videos:** 190 passages, about 23,000 words, from his 25 most-watched videos: every video on his company's channel above 50,000 views (10 videos, from 381k down to 53k, all hosted by him), plus his personal channel's top 15. Added the same day as the call passages, at his direction, because hosted video is his strongest presentational speech. Captions carry word errors; cadence is the signal, wording is checked before anything is quoted.
- **Tier 2:** his dictated explanations for content, verbatim (see the case study for the one that matters most).
- **Tier 3:** launch texts he personally approved.
- **Tier 4:** 93 published posts, labeled CONTAMINATED in the bank header.

## The failure that forced the rebuild

The first version of the bank led with the published posts. It was the obvious design: posts are finished, public, and easy to collect. A launch draft built on it was rejected the same morning with "This isn't in my voice," and the author then identified the root cause himself: many of those posts had been AI-generated by older workflows. The bank was teaching the model its own earlier output. The published set was demoted to tier 4, quarantined in writing, and the bank was rebuilt spoken-first.

## The baseline it has to beat

Kill-rate ledger, pre-rebuild: two full-text voice rejections in three days (a launch draft on Nov 12, another on Nov 14), both drafts having passed every word-level check the operation ran. Post-rebuild, the ledger is reported weekly; the method's claim is falsifiable in exactly one number, and this is it.
