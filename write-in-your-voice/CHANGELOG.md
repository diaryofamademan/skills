# Changelog

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-14

Improvement pass from a cold field test of v1.0.0 (three draft runs against a production bank). Four fixes: (1) the tier 3 rule is now stated in Procedure B — approved texts may source a draft when no spoken or dictated passage covers the move, and the run must declare that sourcing, because the cadence will be the person's written voice, not their spoken one; (2) the word gate gains an accounting rule — every flag is rewritten away or justified in one line in the run's return, and a justified count is not a failure; (3) the bank header now carries the person's channel conventions, so register rules per channel travel with the bank instead of living in a separate private file; (4) the read-aloud anchor is chosen for the piece type — for a launch, the sources behind the most recent approved launch outrank a generic tier 1 pair.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-14

First public release. The method was built in production on the author's own content operation and ships with its evidence: the source-bank build process (gather speech, purge other voices, cut and score passages, tier the bank with published posts quarantined, wire it into drafting, maintain it against a kill-rate ledger), the drafting procedure (compose from the person's own sentences; no source means a dictation prompt, never a composed draft), the word gate and the three-check shape gate, the priority rule (voice outranks register defaults), and the launch pair case study in which the rejected draft scored cleaner on the word gate than the approved one.
