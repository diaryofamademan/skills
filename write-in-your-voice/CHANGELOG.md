# Changelog

## 1.2.6 — 2026-11-21

Sibling calls wired, under the playbook rule: a skill calls the sibling skills its work needs, by name, at the step that needs them. The four-skill pass shaped this skill at build time, but the procedure itself named no calls — a run could complete without ever taking them. Now they are steps: comms at drafting, stop-the-scroll for public posts.

## 1.2.5 — 2026-11-19

The word gate hardens on justifications. A flag may now be justified only by the person's own attested speech: their verbatim phrase, their corpus, a term they were quoting. A skill's own house terms — its structural labels and method vocabulary — never justify a flag; when the only defense of a flagged word is the skill's own terminology, the flag stands and the word is rewritten into the person's plain words. Caught on a launch draft that carried two of the skill's house terms, each defended by citing the skill's locked terminology; checked against the author's corpus, one word appears zero times in their speech and the other only as the skill's structural label, never in their words.

## 1.2.4 — 2026-11-19

The gates now end with the called-in writing pass. After the voice gates, the exact final text runs the work-humanizer skill in rewrite mode as a final register check, then the writing-styles skill's plain rules. The pass is subordinate to the priority rule: any change that fights the source bank loses, because the person's own sentences outrank the pass, always. Facts and figures never change in the pass, and the draft is not finished until the pass has run.

## 1.2.2 — 2026-11-16

Public copy rewritten in plain spoken words; no method changes. Part of the eight-skill plain-copy pass Ian directed on 2026-11-16, after the skill pages were found to read as robotic.

## 1.2.1 — 2026-11-16

The shape gate gains a fourth check, the numbers check: every figure in a draft is written the way the person says it out loud, rounded to the spoken form ("more than 304,000 emails," "$307 billion," "about 3 in 4"); a raw precise count (304,174) or a decimal abbreviation ($307.0B) fails. The rule already ran in the author's production pipeline but was not written in the gates, and a launch draft printed 304,174 raw behind them. Exact figures live in the source material; the draft carries the number as speech. No other method changes.

## 1.2.0 — 2026-11-16

A grammar check joins the gates, beside the word gate: on the exact final text, agreement, articles, punctuation, hyphenation, and parallelism are checked, and every finding is accounted for the way word-gate flags are — fixed, or justified in one line in the run's return. The person's own attested habits are the standing justification, never errors to be corrected into standard prose; the check exists for the mistakes the person would call mistakes. The gate exists because finished copy passed the word and shape gates carrying a plain agreement error — both gates judge how a text sounds, and neither judged whether it was correct. No other method changes.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-14

Improvement pass from a cold field test of v1.0.0 (three draft runs against a production bank). Four fixes: (1) the tier 3 rule is now stated in Procedure B — approved texts may source a draft when no spoken or dictated passage covers the move, and the run must declare that sourcing, because the cadence will be the person's written voice, not their spoken one; (2) the word gate gains an accounting rule — every flag is rewritten away or justified in one line in the run's return, and a justified count is not a failure; (3) the bank header now carries the person's channel conventions, so register rules per channel travel with the bank instead of living in a separate private file; (4) the read-aloud anchor is chosen for the piece type — for a launch, the sources behind the most recent approved launch outrank a generic tier 1 pair.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-14

First public release. The method was built in production on the author's own content operation and ships with its evidence: the source-bank build process (gather speech, purge other voices, cut and score passages, tier the bank with published posts quarantined, wire it into drafting, maintain it against a kill-rate ledger), the drafting procedure (compose from the person's own sentences; no source means a dictation prompt, never a composed draft), the word gate and the three-check shape gate, the priority rule (voice outranks register defaults), and the launch pair case study in which the rejected draft scored cleaner on the word gate than the approved one.
